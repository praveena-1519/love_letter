My Dear Love ❤️

Our friendship started in February 2022.

From that day till now, our journey has been full of memories—smiles, misunderstandings, fights, and many arguments too. But through every situation, we learned something important: how to understand each other, how to accept our mistakes, and how to stand together.

In August 2022, we took our first step into love.

That phase was not easy for us. We struggled a lot, faced many challenges, and sometimes felt tired emotionally. But still, we never gave up on each other. No matter how hard things became, our hearts always chose to stay together.

On October 17, 2024, we got engaged.

That day made our love stronger and more meaningful. Even after that, we had some misunderstandings, but we supported each other and kept our relationship safe with trust and patience.
On February 11, 2025, we became one.
And finally,

on November 15, 2025, we got married.

That was the most beautiful day of my life. Our love story is not just about happy moments—it is about struggles, burdens, tears, emotions, and still choosing each other every single time.
All these struggles became our bridge, my dear hubby, bringing us closer and stronger.
I am so proud of our journey and grateful to have you in my life.

Happy 3rd Month Anniversary, my love. Forever yours ❤️

Every moment with you is my favorite story 💕
